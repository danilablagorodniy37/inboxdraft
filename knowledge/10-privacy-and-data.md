# Privacy and data protection

## Our commitments

Wrenfield Accounting is registered with the Information Commissioner's Office and handles your personal data under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018. We collect only what we need to do your accounts and tax, and we never sell or share your data for marketing.

## What we keep and for how long

We keep client records, including identity documents, tax returns and working papers, for 6 years after the end of our engagement, which is the period HMRC can look back at. After that the records are securely deleted or shredded. Prospective clients who do not become clients have their details deleted after 12 months.

## Where your data is stored

Client records live in the client portal and our practice software, both hosted in UK data centres. Email is hosted in the UK and the EU. Paper records are kept in locked cabinets in the office and scanned into the portal.

## Who sees your data

Your named accountant and the staff working on your file. We share data with HMRC and Companies House to file your returns, with your previous accountant for professional clearance, and with the software providers we use to prepare your work, all under written agreements. We do not pass your data to anyone else without your permission unless the law requires it.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete data we no longer need to keep. Email privacy@wrenfield.example and we will respond within 1 month. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how we handle your data.

## Email and phone safety

- We never ask for online banking passwords, card PINs or full card numbers by email or phone.
- We will not ask you to move money to a new bank account by email. If you receive such a request that appears to come from us, phone the office on 01632 960 123 before doing anything.
- Please do not send identity documents by email; use the portal.
- If you would like us to encrypt emails to you, ask your accountant and we will set it up.

## Software-assisted drafting

Some replies to email enquiries are drafted with software from the firm's own documents. Before any text is processed by that software, personal details such as names, email addresses, phone numbers and postcodes are replaced with placeholders, and they are put back only in the finished draft on our own systems. A member of staff reads, edits and sends every reply.
