# Appointments and meetings

## Booking

You can book by phone on 01632 960 123, by email to hello@wrenfield.example, or online at wrenfield.example/book, where you can see live availability. We confirm every booking by email with a calendar invitation.

## Your first meeting

New enquiries start with a free 20-minute call, by phone or video, so we can understand what you need and give you a fee in writing. There is no obligation to go ahead.

## Meeting options

- In person at the office, Unit 4, Riverside Court, Guildford.
- Video call on Microsoft Teams or Google Meet; we send the link with the confirmation.
- Phone call.

Standard meeting slots are Monday to Friday between 9:00 and 17:00. Evening slots are available on Tuesday and Thursday until 19:00 by arrangement. Saturday appointments are available from 9:00 to 13:00 by prior arrangement only.

## Changing or cancelling

Please give us at least 24 hours' notice to cancel or move a meeting, by phone or email. For paid meetings (advice sessions at £110 per hour and year-end review meetings) a cancellation with less than 24 hours' notice, or a missed meeting, is charged at £40. There is no charge for cancelling a free initial call, but we are grateful for the notice.

## Getting here and parking

The office is a 5-minute walk from Guildford railway station. There are 2 visitor parking spaces outside the office; if they are taken, use the Riverside car park next door and we will validate 2 hours of parking at reception.

## What to bring

For a first meeting, bring your photo ID and proof of address so we can complete the identity checks on the day. For a year-end meeting, bring any records you have not yet uploaded to the portal. Nothing else is needed; we will have your file ready.
