# The client portal

## What it is for

Every client gets access to the Wrenfield client portal, where you can upload documents, sign engagement letters and accounts electronically, see your deadlines and message your accountant. It is the safest way to send us records and the fastest way to get your work started.

## Getting access

We send a portal invitation to your email address when you become a client. The link in the invitation is valid for 7 days; if it has expired, ask us for a new one. You choose your own password at first login.

## Security

The portal uses two-factor authentication: after your password you enter a 6-digit code from an authenticator app or a text message. Data is stored in UK data centres and encrypted in transit and at rest. Staff access is logged. If you think your login has been compromised, call us on 01632 960 123 and we will reset it straight away.

## Uploading documents

- Accepted file types: PDF, JPG, PNG, HEIC, XLSX, CSV and DOCX.
- Maximum file size: 25 MB per file. There is no limit on the number of files.
- Photos of receipts taken on a phone are fine as long as the figures are readable.
- Put files in the folder for the right tax year; if you are not sure, upload them to "Unsorted" and we will file them.

You get an email confirmation for every upload, and your accountant is notified the same day.

## Electronic signatures

Engagement letters, accounts and tax returns are sent to the portal for electronic signature. You will get an email when something is waiting for you. Signatures are time-stamped and stored with the document. If you would rather sign on paper, let us know and we will post the documents.

## Problems logging in

Use "Forgotten password" on the login page to reset your password. If you no longer have access to the phone or authenticator app you used for two-factor authentication, call the office and we will verify your identity and reset the second factor. We cannot do this by email.
