# Frequently asked questions

## Do I need an accountant for a simple tax return?

Not always. If your only income is from one job and you have no other income or reliefs to claim, you may not need to file a return at all. If you do need to file, a basic return with us is from £180 and includes checking that you have claimed everything you are entitled to.

## How long will my tax return take?

Within 10 working days of receiving all your documents, or 15 working days in January. We will tell you what you owe and when, and we file only after you have approved the return through the portal.

## Can I switch to you from another accountant?

Yes, and there is no charge for the switch. Once you have signed our engagement letter we write to your previous accountant for professional clearance and ask them for your records. You do not need to contact them yourself, though a short note from you speeds things up.

## Do you work with clients outside Surrey?

Yes. About a third of our clients are elsewhere in the UK and work with us entirely through the portal, video calls and phone. We cannot act for clients who need returns filed in another country.

## Which software should I use?

We recommend Xero for most businesses. We also support QuickBooks Online and FreeAgent. We can set you up, move your data across and train you, charged at £110 per hour.

## What if I miss a deadline?

Contact us straight away. HMRC charges an automatic £100 penalty for a late Self Assessment return, and the penalties grow after 3 months, so filing even a few days late is much better than waiting. If the delay was caused by something outside your control we can help you appeal the penalty.

## How do you keep my data safe?

Records are stored in the client portal in UK data centres with two-factor authentication, and we keep them for 6 years after the end of our engagement, as HMRC requires. See our privacy notice for details.

## Do you use AI?

Yes, in a limited way. Replies to email enquiries and some routine letters may be drafted with software from our own documents, and a member of staff reads, edits and sends every reply. Your personal details are removed before any text is processed by software outside the firm. We never use software to make decisions about your tax affairs.

## Can I pay in instalments?

Fixed-fee clients pay monthly by Direct Debit, which spreads the cost across the year. One-off work is invoiced on completion and due within 14 days; if that is difficult, speak to us before the due date and we will agree a plan.
