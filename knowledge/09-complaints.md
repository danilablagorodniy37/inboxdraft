# Complaints policy

## How to complain

If something has gone wrong, please tell us. Email complaints@wrenfield.example or write to Helen Marsh, Practice Principal, Wrenfield Accounting, Unit 4, Riverside Court, Guildford, GU1 9ZZ. You can also raise a complaint with your accountant by phone, and they will log it for you.

## What happens next

1. We acknowledge every complaint within 2 working days and tell you who is handling it. Complaints are always handled by someone other than the accountant involved.
2. We investigate and send a full written response within 10 working days. If we need longer, we tell you why and when to expect the response.
3. If we got something wrong, we say so, put it right, and tell you what we have changed to stop it happening again. Where our error caused a penalty or interest, we cover the cost.

## If you are not satisfied

If you are not happy with our response, you can ask for the complaint to be reviewed by the practice principal, and after that you can refer it to our professional body, whose details we give you with our final response.

## Fees in dispute

While a complaint about a fee is being investigated we do not chase the disputed amount, and no late-payment charges apply to it.

## Record keeping

We keep a log of every complaint and review it quarterly to improve how we work. The log is kept for 6 years.
