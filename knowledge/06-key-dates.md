# Key dates and deadlines

These are the main HMRC and Companies House deadlines as they stand at the time of writing. We confirm the exact dates for your own affairs in writing and send a reminder 6 weeks before each deadline. Please check gov.uk for the current rules if you are reading this later.

## Self Assessment

- Register for Self Assessment by 5 October following the end of the tax year in which you first had untaxed income.
- Paper tax return: 31 October following the end of the tax year.
- Online tax return: 31 January following the end of the tax year.
- Pay the tax you owe by 31 January.
- If your bill is over £1,000, you usually make 2 payments on account for the following year: 31 January and 31 July.
- A late online return gets an automatic £100 penalty from HMRC, with further penalties after 3 months.

To guarantee filing on time, send us your complete records at least 4 weeks before the deadline, so by 31 December for the online return.

## Limited companies

- Annual accounts to Companies House: 9 months after the company's year end.
- Corporation tax payment: 9 months and 1 day after the year end.
- Corporation tax return (CT600) to HMRC: 12 months after the year end.
- Confirmation statement: at least once every 12 months, within 14 days of the review date.

## VAT

- VAT returns and payment: 1 month and 7 days after the end of the VAT period. For a quarter ending 31 March, the return and payment are due by 7 May.
- Payments by Direct Debit are collected 3 working days after the return deadline.

## Payroll

- PAYE and National Insurance: pay HMRC by the 22nd of the following month when paying electronically.
- Give employees their P60 by 31 May.
- File P11Ds and pay Class 1A National Insurance by 6 July.

## Capital gains on UK property

- Report and pay within 60 days of completion when you sell a UK residential property that is not your main home.
