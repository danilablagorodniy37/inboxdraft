# Fees

All fees are shown excluding VAT. We are VAT registered, so VAT at 20% is added to every invoice. Fixed fees are agreed in writing in the engagement letter before any work starts, and we tell you in advance if something falls outside the agreed scope.

## Self Assessment tax returns

- Basic return (employment income, pensions, savings interest, dividends): from £180.
- Return with 1 rental property: £240. Each additional rental property adds £40.
- Sole trader or partner, including preparing the business accounts: from £300.
- Capital gains computation added to a return: from £120.

The fee includes checking your figures, filing the return with HMRC, and telling you what to pay and when.

## Limited companies

- Annual accounts and corporation tax return (CT600): from £750 per year.
- Confirmation statement: £60 plus the Companies House filing fee of £34.
- Company formation: £150, including the Companies House fee, with the company usually registered within 2 working days.
- Registered office address service: £120 per year.
- Director's personal Self Assessment return: from £180, as above.

## Bookkeeping, VAT and payroll

- Bookkeeping: from £95 per month for up to 100 transactions a month, or £150 per month for up to 300 transactions. We work in Xero, QuickBooks Online or FreeAgent.
- VAT returns: £60 per quarter when we do your bookkeeping, or from £120 per quarter if you keep your own books and we review and file the return.
- Payroll: £25 per month for up to 5 employees, then £4 per additional payslip. This includes payslips, RTI submissions to HMRC, P60s and leaver forms.
- Auto-enrolment pension set-up with a workplace pension provider: £150 one-off.
- CIS monthly returns for contractors: £35 per month.
- Management accounts (quarterly profit and loss, balance sheet and a short commentary): from £200 per quarter.

## Advice and one-off work

- Ad hoc advice and meetings: £110 per hour, billed in 15-minute units.
- Your first 20-minute call with us is free of charge, whether or not you become a client.
- Xero or QuickBooks training: £110 per hour, usually 2 hours.

## Paying us

- Invoices are due within 14 days of the invoice date.
- Monthly fixed-fee clients pay by Direct Debit on the 1st of each month.
- We accept bank transfer and card payments. We do not accept cash.
- Taking over from a previous accountant is free of charge: we write to them for professional clearance and collect your records.
