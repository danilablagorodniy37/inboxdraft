# New clients and what to send us

## Before we can start

By law we must verify the identity of every client before we do any work (anti-money-laundering checks). We need:

- Photo ID: a passport or a photocard driving licence.
- Proof of address: a utility bill, council tax bill or bank statement dated within the last 3 months.
- A signed engagement letter, which we send through the client portal for electronic signature.

You can show the ID in person at the office or upload a clear photo of it through the portal. We do not accept ID by email.

## Self Assessment: documents for your tax return

- Your Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR), the 10-digit number HMRC gave you when you registered.
- Your National Insurance number.
- P60 from each employer, and a P45 if you left a job during the year.
- P11D if you received benefits such as a company car or medical insurance.
- Bank or building society interest statements and dividend vouchers.
- For rental property: rent received, letting agent statements, mortgage interest statements and receipts for repairs and other costs.
- For self-employment: sales invoices, expense receipts and bank statements for the business account for the whole tax year (6 April to 5 April).
- Pension contributions, Gift Aid donations and student loan statements.
- Details of your previous accountant, if you had one.

If you do not have a P60, your employer can give you a copy, or you can take the figures from your HMRC personal tax account online.

## Limited companies: documents for your accounts

- Certificate of incorporation and the company's Companies House authentication code.
- Last year's accounts and corporation tax computation, if the company is not new.
- VAT registration number and VAT returns filed in the year.
- Bank statements for all company accounts for the whole accounting year.
- Sales invoices, purchase invoices and expense receipts, or access to your bookkeeping software.
- Payroll records, including P60s and P11Ds.
- Details of any loans, hire purchase agreements and assets bought or sold.

## How to get documents to us

- Preferred: upload through the client portal (see the portal guide). Files up to 25 MB each.
- Email attachments to hello@wrenfield.example, up to 20 MB in total per email. Please do not send ID documents by email.
- By post or by hand to Unit 4, Riverside Court, Guildford, GU1 9ZZ. We scan paper records and return them at your next visit, or by post on request.

Please never send us online banking passwords or card PINs. We do not need them and we will never ask for them.
