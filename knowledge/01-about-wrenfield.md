# About Wrenfield Accounting

Wrenfield Accounting Ltd is a fictional accountancy practice used as the demo knowledge base for inboxdraft. Any resemblance to a real firm is coincidental.

## Who we are

Wrenfield Accounting is a small accountancy practice in Guildford, Surrey, founded in 2014. We look after sole traders, landlords, contractors and limited companies with up to about 30 employees. Most of our clients are in Surrey and south-west London, and we work with clients across the UK remotely through our client portal, video calls and phone.

## The team

We are a team of 6: 3 chartered accountants, 1 bookkeeper, 1 payroll administrator and 1 office manager. Every client has a named accountant who is their main point of contact. The practice principal is Helen Marsh.

## Where to find us

Our office is at Unit 4, Riverside Court, Guildford, Surrey, GU1 9ZZ, a 5-minute walk from Guildford railway station. There are 2 visitor parking spaces outside the office; if they are taken, the Riverside car park next door charges by the hour and we can validate 2 hours of parking for client meetings.

## Opening hours

The office is open Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 17:30. Phone lines are answered from 9:00 to 17:00. Saturday appointments are available from 9:00 to 13:00 by prior arrangement only. We are closed on Sundays and bank holidays. In the last 2 weeks of January the office stays open until 19:00 on weekdays for the Self Assessment deadline.
