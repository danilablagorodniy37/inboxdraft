# Services

## What we do

- Self Assessment tax returns for employees, directors, landlords, sole traders and partners.
- Annual accounts and corporation tax for limited companies.
- Bookkeeping in Xero, QuickBooks Online and FreeAgent, with receipts captured through Dext.
- VAT registration, VAT returns and advice on the flat rate scheme.
- Payroll, including RTI submissions, P60s, P11Ds and auto-enrolment pension duties.
- CIS returns and verification for contractors in construction.
- Company formation, confirmation statements and registered office address.
- Management accounts and cash flow forecasts.
- Capital gains tax computations, including the 60-day reporting of UK residential property sales.
- Making Tax Digital set-up and quarterly submissions.

## What we do not do

We do not give investment, mortgage or pension advice, and we do not prepare tax returns for countries other than the UK. If you need an independent financial adviser or an overseas tax specialist, we can suggest firms we have worked with, but we do not take a fee for the introduction.

## Software we support

Xero is our preferred platform and most of our clients use it. We also support QuickBooks Online and FreeAgent. If you use spreadsheets or another system we can still take you on, and we will suggest a move to cloud software if it would save you money.

## Making Tax Digital for Income Tax

From April 2026, self-employed people and landlords with gross income over £50,000 must keep digital records and send quarterly updates to HMRC under Making Tax Digital for Income Tax. The threshold drops to £30,000 from April 2027. We set up the software, link it to HMRC and send the quarterly updates as part of our bookkeeping service. Check the current thresholds on gov.uk, as they can change.

## Capital gains on property

If you sell a UK residential property that is not your main home, you must report the gain and pay the tax within 60 days of completion. We prepare and file the 60-day return; the fee is from £120 when added to an existing Self Assessment engagement.

## Turnaround times

- Self Assessment return: within 10 working days of receiving all your documents. In January this rises to 15 working days, so please send everything by 31 December if you can.
- Limited company accounts: within 4 weeks of receiving the year-end records.
- VAT returns: filed at least 3 working days before the due date when we have your records by the 10th of the month.
- Payroll: payslips sent 2 working days before pay day when we receive the hours by the agreed cut-off.
